About us

Learning with purpose. Growing with confidence.

Royal International Schools is a private school in Hai Amarat, Juba. We offer a welcoming environment where students can learn, grow and work towards their ambitions.

We believe every student should be known, encouraged and challenged. Our aim is to help each learner build strong foundations, ask thoughtful questions and take pride in their progress.

From Preschool through Secondary, we bring academic learning and personal growth together to prepare students for the opportunities ahead.

Our philosophy

At Royal International Schools, we believe every child deserves an opportunity to discover their potential, develop confidence and build a strong foundation for lifelong success.

A supportive learning environment

We are committed to creating a supportive learning environment where academic development, creativity, discipline and personal growth come together to prepare learners for a changing world.

More than knowledge

Education is more than the acquisition of knowledge. It is about developing character, encouraging curiosity, discovering talents and equipping young people with the skills to make meaningful contributions to society.

At Royal International Schools, we aim to nurture learners who are confident, responsible, creative and prepared to embrace future opportunities.

The whole child

We believe that quality education should develop the whole child. Academic knowledge must go hand in hand with discipline, integrity, confidence, creativity and respect for others.

Our educational philosophy focuses on creating opportunities for learners to grow intellectually, socially and personally.
